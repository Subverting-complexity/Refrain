# Refrain — Privacy Policy

_Last updated: 3 July 2026_

Refrain ("the app") is an audio loop player developed by Subverting Complexity.
This policy explains what the app does and does not do with your information.
It is short because the app is simple: **Refrain collects no personal data.**

## Data we collect

**None.** Refrain does not collect, transmit, sell, or share any personal
information. There are no user accounts, no sign-up, and no login.

## How your content is handled

- **Audio you import** is read directly from your device for playback and
  waveform display. It stays on your device and is never uploaded to us or to
  any third party.
- **Your loops, segments, bookmarks, and settings** are stored locally on your
  device (in the app's own storage). They are never sent off the device.

## Network use

Refrain works fully offline. The app makes no network requests for the purpose
of collecting data and contains no analytics, advertising, or crash-reporting
services.

## Permissions

Refrain requests only the access it needs to let you choose and play local
audio files. It does not access your contacts, location, or camera, and does
not use the microphone for recording.

## Children's privacy

Because the app collects no data, it collects no data from children. Refrain is
suitable for all ages.

## Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted at this URL with a
new "Last updated" date.
