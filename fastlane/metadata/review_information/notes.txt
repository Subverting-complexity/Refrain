Refrain is an offline audio looper: import an audio file, set A and B
markers on the waveform, loop that section on repeat, save it as a
segment to reload later. For musicians, language learners, dancers and
transcribers who learn by repetition.

No account, login, credentials or purchase required. Nothing is gated.

To test: launch, tap "Import Audio" and choose an MP3, WAV, AAC or M4A
file, open the track, drag the A/B markers, press play. Also has skip
intervals, a count-in timer, bookmarks, volume control and folders.

No permission prompts of any kind (no location, contacts, camera,
microphone, photos, or App Tracking Transparency). File access is only
through the system file picker on Import.

No external services: no network requests, no backend, no analytics, no
ads, no third-party SDKs. Everything is stored locally (SQLite and the
file system). Privacy label is Data Not Collected.

One language (English), no server, identical behaviour in every region.
Not a regulated industry. The only bundled audio is a short click tone
for the metronome count-in; all other audio is user-imported.
